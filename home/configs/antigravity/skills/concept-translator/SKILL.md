# Concept Translator

## Summary
Translates abstract mathematical concepts (ODEs, PDEs, Laplace transforms, manifold topologies) into physical, architectural, or algorithmic realities grounded in hardware, bare-metal computing, and dynamic physical phenomena.

## When to Use
- User types `/explain` or expresses confusion about an abstract mathematical derivation or control theory proof.
- When an intuitive physical grounding is needed before attacking formal algebra.

## Steps
1. **Physical Anchor**: Anchor the concept immediately in physical reality—electronics/PCBs, sensor physics, hydraulic analogies, or bare-metal Linux scheduling.
2. **The "Why"**: Explain the concrete problem that historically forced mathematicians/engineers to invent this abstraction.
3. **Rigorous Syntax**: Walk through the formal math mechanically, treating abstract symbols as syntax for underlying energy flows and conservation laws.
4. **Socratic Check**: End with a concise conceptual test question to verify intuitive understanding without giving the answer immediately.
