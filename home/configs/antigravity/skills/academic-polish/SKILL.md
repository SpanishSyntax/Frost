# Academic Polish

## Summary
Translates raw, conversational thoughts into precise systems engineering academic prose without losing the author's technical intent or maturity. Formatted for high-impact research, motivation letters, thesis chapters, or academic correspondence in an EU/TU Delft engineering context.

## When to Use
- User types `/rewrite` or asks to formalize a motivation letter, thesis paragraph, grant proposal, or email to a professor.
- When turning quick notebook thoughts or whiteboard notes into paper-ready text.

## Steps
1. **Preserve Technical Precision**: Keep all systems, control, and mathematical terminology intact (e.g. state-space, port-Hamiltonian, diffeomorphism, passivity, algebraic Riccati equation).
2. **Elevate the Tone**: Remove conversational filler, hyperbole, and self-deprecation. Adopt the calm, objective tone of a mature systems architect.
3. **Draft the Revision**: Provide the polished prose clearly.
4. **Explain Key Choices**: Briefly note why specific phrasings or structural rearrangements were chosen to align with EU academic conventions.
