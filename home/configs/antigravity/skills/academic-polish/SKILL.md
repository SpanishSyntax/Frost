# Academic Polish

## Summary
Translates raw, conversational thoughts and informal technical notes into publication-grade systems engineering academic prose without altering the author's underlying technical intent, mathematics, or conceptual maturity. Designed for high-impact research papers, motivation letters, master/doctoral thesis chapters, and scholarly correspondence within European (e.g. TU Delft, ETH Zurich, TU Munich) systems and control engineering conventions.

## When to Use
- The user asks to polish, rewrite, formalize, or elevate a motivation letter, thesis paragraph, research paper section, grant proposal, or email to a professor.
- The user provides informal bullet points, whiteboard notes, or conversational explanations and asks to convert them into formal, paper-ready academic prose.
- The user seeks feedback on tone, precision, and adherence to European academic writing norms.

## Operational Procedures

1. **Preserve Mathematical & Architectural Precision**:
   - Keep all domain-specific systems engineering, control theory, and optimization terminology intact and mathematically exact (e.g., state-space representations, port-Hamiltonian mechanics, diffeomorphism, Lyapunov stability, passivity indices, algebraic Riccati equations).
   - Never replace rigorous mathematical terms with generic synonyms (e.g., do not replace "affine in control" with "linear-like").

2. **Elevate Tone & Eliminate Conversational Noise**:
   - Strip colloquialisms, self-deprecating remarks, hedging filler words (e.g., "basically", "sort of", "kind of"), and breathless marketing hype.
   - Maintain the calm, measured, and objective voice of a senior systems architect or faculty researcher.

3. **Structure for Clarity & Flow**:
   - Ensure deductive paragraph structures: state the thesis or finding first, followed by supporting mechanisms, mathematical justification, and physical consequences.
   - Use active voice for methodological decisions ("We formulate the optimal control problem...") and passive or neutral voice for physical phenomena ("Energy dissipation occurs along the boundary layer...").

4. **Provide Contextual Rationale**:
   - Provide the polished draft clearly formatted in GitHub-style markdown.
   - Conclude with a brief, high-level summary of key syntactic or structural adjustments made, explaining why specific terminology aligns with formal European academic conventions.
